About me

Hi. My name is Remée Hutchings, I am currently a student at Bristol School of Acting studying for a BA in Technical Theatre Arts. My passion for theatre started in year 7 when I joined a small, school-ran club for operating theatre equipment. Although the equipment we had was limited, it lit the spark for my career journey into the modern world of technical theatre and live event production.

Beyond technical theatre, I have many interests and passions, like my love for music (listening and playing) or ceramics. My favourite muscial I have seen was The Phantom of The Opera on the West End and my favourite play was Studio Ghibli's - Spirited Away. I have travelled to various places in Europe like Cologne and Skopelos with my family, and I went to my first ever concert in July 2024.
